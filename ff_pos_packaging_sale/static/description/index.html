POS Packaging Sale

Sell by Carton & Box — stock keeps moving in base Units

Let your cashiers sell products by commercial Packaging (Carton, Box, Pack…) in Odoo 19 Community Point of Sale, while the actual stock movement stays in the product's base Unit. One product, one barcode — no separate products, no BoM, no UoM changes.

Features

  • Packaging POS fields on the Odoo 19 packaging model: Available in POS, POS Name, POS Price, Default in POS, Sequence.
  • Per-product behavior: Default Packaging / Ask Every Time / Unit by Default — or inherit the POS config default.
  • Fixed packaging prices independent of the unit price (Carton = 290, not 24 × 15), with a documented fallback: contained qty × pricelist unit price.
  • Structured data on order lines and invoice lines (no description-string hacks): packaging, count, contained qty, base-UoM qty — ready for reporting.
  • Refunds keep the packaging semantics: refund 1 Carton, stock gets 24 Units back.
  • Offline-safe: packaging data is loaded with the POS payload and synced back with the order.
  • Multi-company safe: packagings keep their company; POS loads only packagings of the loaded products.
  • Inventory / COGS untouched: stock moves stay in the base UoM; valuation, FIFO/AVCO and margins use the converted quantity.
  • Automated tests covering stock conversion, taxes, refunds, sync fields and all business constraints.

Configuration

  1. Open Point of Sale → Configuration → POS Packagings.
  2. For each packaging: choose the contained unit, set the POS name & price, tick Available in POS (+ Default in POS if needed).
  3. On the product form → Point of Sale page: choose the Packaging Selection Behavior, or leave it as Per POS Config.
